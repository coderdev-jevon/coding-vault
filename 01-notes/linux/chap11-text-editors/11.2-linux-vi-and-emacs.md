## * Introduction to `vi`
- It is a ==standard tool== installed on Linux distribution.
- Actual program installed on system is `vim` which stands for ==Vi Improved==
- Pronounced as ==vee-eye==
- GNOME offers `gvim` and KDE offers `kvim` as the graphical interfaces/
