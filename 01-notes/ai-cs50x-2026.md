## * Prompt Engineering

What role you give to the agent and how you would like them to be

#### ** System Prompt Hijacking / Prompt Injection
Modern models have anti-injection filters and instruction prioritization logic to block this, and it does not modify the original built-in system. It is possible if you control the API call code.

## * Machine Learning
Learn to solve problems by being trained by massive amount of data and look for patterns to solve the next problem.

#### ** Reinforcement Learning
Do more of one thing and less of the other.
#### ** Supervised Learning
With the help of human to identify and use it as data
#### ** Unsupervised Learning
Without the help of human, due to large data or large cost
#### ** Deep Learning
Solving problems by code
#### ** Neural Networks
From input to output, from raw data to prediction, automatically discovers features or connections step by step and at the end discover the right weight of each connections and create accurate prediction.
#### ** Large Language Models (LLMs)
Rubber Duck, Chat GPT, Claude, Gemini


#### ** Generative Pre-trained Transformers
GPT is a type of AI neural network that writes new text word-by-word.

**Transformer**, look at all words in text and understand relationship
**Pre-trained**, first self studies billions of pages
**Generative**, generates sequences word by word

#### ** Hallucinations
AI predicts what text should logically come next, but sometimes outputs is completely fake or made-up, just probability.
