# If I were mentoring you personally

Given everything you've shared over the past months, I would make **one important adjustment** compared to many AI roadmaps.

I would **not** rush you into machine learning after data structures and algorithms.

Instead, I'd spend a long time making sure you have a deep understanding of **systems**: operating systems, networking, distributed systems, concurrency, and performance. Those are the skills that distinguish AI systems engineers from engineers who can only train or fine-tune models.

Modern AI increasingly depends on **large-scale systems engineering**. Training and serving frontier models is as much about distributed computing, networking, storage, scheduling, and optimization as it is about neural networks.

So if your ambition is to create real impact on the AI world—not just use AI frameworks—I'd prioritize becoming excellent at the intersection of **computer science, systems engineering, and machine learning**. That's a foundation that will remain valuable even as models, libraries, and techniques continue to evolve.
